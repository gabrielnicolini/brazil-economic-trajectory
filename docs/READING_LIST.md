# Reading list

One entry per topic, with the most authoritative open link available.
Links for later phases are added when each phase starts.

## Phase 1 - Causal inference basics

- Angrist and Pischke, *Mastering 'Metrics* (Princeton University Press, 2014).
  - Book record: https://ideas.repec.org/b/pup/pbooks/10363.html
  - Introduction (free, publisher PDF): http://assets.press.princeton.edu/chapters/i10363.pdf
  - Chapter 1, Randomized Trials (free, publisher PDF): http://press.princeton.edu/chapters/s10363.pdf

## Phase 5 - Counterfactuals

- Abadie (2021), "Using Synthetic Controls: Feasibility, Data Requirements,
  and Methodological Aspects", *Journal of Economic Literature* 59(2).
  - Open-access published version (MIT DSpace): https://dspace.mit.edu/handle/1721.1/144417
  - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1257/jel.20191450

## Phase 7 - Game theory of monetary policy

- Barro and Gordon (1983), "Rules, Discretion and Reputation in a Model of
  Monetary Policy", NBER Working Paper 1079 (published in the *Journal of
  Monetary Economics* 12(1)).
  - NBER page with PDF: https://www.nber.org/papers/W1079
